The problem, in plain terms

When a recurring payment fails, most systems just try again a few times and hope. That wastes attempts on payments that were never going to succeed, and misses the ones that would have — if given the right amount of time and the right response.

✕ The usual approach

Retry immediately, a few times, the same way — regardless of why it failed.

✓ What this agent does

Diagnose the real cause, then respond with a retry, a reminder, or human escalation — whichever actually fits.

How it works

Five steps, every time a payment fails — fully automatic, fully logged.

1

Detect

A recurring payment fails to go through.

2

Diagnose

The agent figures out why: not enough balance, a bank glitch, a limit breach, or the customer cancelled the payment altogether.

3

Decide

Based on the cause, it picks the right response — never more than what India's payment rules allow.

4

Act

It retries at the right time, sends a reminder, or hands it to a person — whichever fits.

5

Record

Every decision is logged in plain language, so anyone can see exactly what happened and why.
